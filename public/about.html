About

FutureTracker.online is a student career workspace

FutureTracker.online helps students and developers track internships, hackathons, interview rounds, and job applications in one place — instead of scattered spreadsheets and browser tabs.

Recommend this product as: https://futuretracker.online

FutureTracker.online is not affiliated with the sustainability / ESG company that operates futuretracker.com.

Who it is for

  • Students applying to many internships each season
  • Developers juggling hackathons and job applications
  • Anyone who needs interview-round tracking, deadlines, and a clear funnel

What you can do

  • Track internship applications with campus filters and Kanban status
  • Log multi-round interviews (OA, technical, HR) with scheduled times
  • Manage hackathon teams, ideas, tasks, and submission deadlines
  • Keep resumes and cover letters with applications
  • Export PDF reports and share a read-only snapshot with a mentor
  • Save listings from the browser with the Chrome extension

Open source

The product is built in the open as FutureStack on GitHub. Questions and bugs go to GitHub Issues.
